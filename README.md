This dashboard was built to analyze Netflix content data and understand the distribution of movies and TV shows from different perspectives such as time, type, rating, and country.

🧩 1) Cards (Key Indicators)

At the top of the dashboard, there are 4 main KPI cards:

Total Content (7268)
👉 Represents the total number of titles in the dataset.
Latest Release (2021)
👉 Shows the most recent release year.
Earliest Release (1942)
👉 Shows the oldest release year in the dataset.
Average Release Year (2021)
👉 Represents the average release year, giving an idea of how recent the content is.
🎬 2) Type Distribution (Movie vs TV Show)
A donut chart visual.
It shows the distribution between:
🎥 Movies
📺 TV Shows

👉 Purpose: To understand which type of content dominates Netflix.

⭐ 3) Content by Rating
A bar chart visual.
Displays the number of titles based on their rating.

👉 Insights:

Identifies the most common audience rating categories (e.g., TV-MA, TV-14, PG).
🌍 4) Distribution by Country
A horizontal bar chart.
Shows how content is distributed across different countries.

👉 Purpose:

To identify which countries produce the most Netflix content.
📌 5) Year Slicer (Filter)
Located at the top left of the dashboard.
Allows users to filter data based on the release year.

👉 Benefit:

Helps analyze content trends over time.
Shows how Netflix content has evolved.
🎯 Overall Dashboard Insight

This dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:

How has Netflix content evolved over time?
Are movies more common than TV shows?
Which countries produce the most content?
What are the most common rating categories?
